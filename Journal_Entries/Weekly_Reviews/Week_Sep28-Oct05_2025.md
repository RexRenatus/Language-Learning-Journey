## 💡 Key Reflections & Learnings

### Methodological Innovations

#### Exposure-First Learning Model
**Theory Development** (Sep 28-29):
- Traditional approach: Vocabulary → Grammar → Comprehension
- New approach: Exposure → Pattern Recognition → Intuitive Understanding → Vocabulary
- Rationale: Brain's natural pattern recognition abilities engaged before explicit learning

**Evidence Collection Plan**:
- 100 hours/month reading + listening per language
- Track comprehension improvements without explicit study
- Compare to traditional Anki-only approach from previous Mandarin attempt

#### Two-Stage Memory Architecture
- **Stage 1 - Quizlet**: Rapid exposure, initial pattern formation, low-pressure repetition
- **Stage 2 - Anki**: Spaced repetition for long-term retention once patterns established
- Hypothesis: Early exposure reduces cognitive load during later memorization

#### Multi-Modal Reinforcement
From Sep 29 character recognition insight:
- Previous failure: Anki-only exposure → character confusion
- New approach: Reading + listening + flashcards → multiple pattern reinforcement pathways
- Applied to all three languages going forward
